    Purpose
    
Rethlas-OSV is for mathematicians who want to try more advanced AI tools in mathematical research without spending much time on computer science or engineering. For more serious use, the source code and its terminal interface are recommended: they are more stable, more up to date, and more customizable.

    
The app is developed by Shurui Liu, mostly vibe-coded with Claude Code Fable 5.1, and built upon an early adaptation of OSVerify with Rethlas by Fan Nie. It supports OpenAI models through Codex and, for proving, Anthropic models through Claude Code, on macOS only.

    
Feedback, comments, and suggestions are very welcome. Please contact Shurui Liu at srliu3264@gmail.com.

    Suggestions for users
    
    
      
  1. You need a GPT subscription. Everything stays on your computer: settings, every run with its inputs, reports, and execution records. Model calls go to OpenAI through the Codex CLI under your own account, or, for prove runs that select it, to Anthropic through the Claude Code CLI under your own Claude account. Rethlas-OSV stores no credentials and sends nothing anywhere else.
    2. 
      
  2. Use it responsibly. People hold different views on the future of mathematics and its protocols, but please make sure you intend to do good for the mathematical community and for humanity.
    3. 
      
  3. Monitor the usage on your GPT account. An agentic workflow can be expensive, since it calls the base model continuously.
    4. 
      
  4. Attribute credit properly when generating proofs. A generated solution may not properly credit previous work or ongoing work in the community.
    5. 
      
  5. Get permission before reviewing a paper. Ask the authors, the journal, and any other party involved. Rethlas-OSV only reports detected correctness issues; it makes no judgement of novelty or significance.
    6. 
      
  6. No guarantee of correctness. Like any AI tool in mathematics, Rethlas-OSV can be wrong. READY_FOR_HUMAN is a handoff for a human reader, not a proof certificate.
    7. 
      
  7. Be transparent about generative AI usage. Judge with common sense.
    8. 
      
  8. We deeply appreciate it if you could cite our papers (in the case that Rethlas-OSV helped your work). The entries below are ready to copy.
